# QuickBite Restaurant Partner FAQ

## Becoming a Partner Restaurant
Restaurants can apply to join QuickBite through the "Partner with Us" page by submitting a valid business/food license, NTN (National Tax Number), bank account details, and a sample menu with prices. Onboarding review typically takes 5-7 business days, followed by a menu photography session (provided free of charge in major cities).

## Commission Structure
QuickBite charges a commission of 18-25% per order, depending on the restaurant's category, order volume tier, and whether the restaurant participates in QuickBite marketing promotions. New restaurants receive a reduced commission rate of 15% for their first 60 days on the platform.

## Menu Management
Partner restaurants can update prices, mark items as sold out, and add or remove menu items anytime through the QuickBite Restaurant Partner Dashboard, with changes reflected in the customer app within 5 minutes. Full menu overhauls (new categories, rebranding) should be submitted via the support portal for a re-review of photos and descriptions.

## Order Acceptance and Preparation Time
Restaurants must accept or reject incoming orders within 2 minutes; unaccepted orders are automatically cancelled and offered to the customer as a refund. Restaurants set their own estimated preparation time per order, which should reflect real kitchen conditions — consistently inaccurate preparation times can affect the restaurant's visibility ranking in search results.

## Payouts to Restaurants
Restaurant payouts (order value minus commission) are transferred weekly, every Wednesday, for the previous Monday-Sunday sales period, directly to the restaurant's registered bank account. Detailed order-level statements are available for download from the Partner Dashboard at any time.

## Handling Customer Complaints
If a customer reports an issue with food quality or a missing item, the restaurant is notified and given 48 hours to respond with their side before a refund decision is finalized. Restaurants with a complaint rate above 5% of total orders in a rolling 30-day window are flagged for a quality review call with the Partner Success team.

## Promotional Tools
Restaurants can opt into QuickBite-funded promotions (e.g. "Buy 1 Get 1", featured placement) at no extra commission cost, or fund their own restaurant-specific discounts, which are deducted from the restaurant's payout rather than QuickBite's revenue.
