# Indus Air - Special Assistance, Minors and Medical Travel

Document owner: Customer Care
Last reviewed: 2026-07-02
Applies to: passengers requiring additional support on Indus Air operated flights

## Requesting Assistance

All assistance must be requested at least 48 hours before scheduled departure through
Manage Booking or the Indus Air call centre. Requests made inside 48 hours are handled on
a best effort basis and cannot be guaranteed.

Assistance is provided free of charge. Indus Air does not charge for wheelchairs, guide
animals, oxygen approval or minor escort at the airport.

## Wheelchair and Mobility Assistance

Three service codes are used at the time of booking:

- WCHR: passenger can walk short distances and climb stairs, needs a wheelchair between
  the terminal entrance and the aircraft door.
- WCHS: passenger cannot climb stairs, needs a wheelchair to the aircraft door and a
  ramp or lift to board.
- WCHC: passenger is immobile, needs to be carried to the aircraft seat by trained staff.

Passengers travelling with their own wheelchair carry it free of charge in addition to
the fare baggage allowance. Battery powered wheelchairs must be declared 48 hours before
departure so the battery type can be verified. Wet cell batteries must be disconnected and
the terminals insulated before acceptance.

## Unaccompanied Minors

- Children aged 5 to 11 travelling without an adult aged 18 or over must travel under the
  Unaccompanied Minor (UMNR) service. The service is mandatory for this age group.
- Children aged 12 to 15 may travel alone as ordinary passengers. Parents may optionally
  request the UMNR service for them.
- Children under 5 cannot travel unaccompanied under any circumstances.
- The UMNR service fee is PKR 5,000 per child per sector on domestic flights and
  PKR 12,000 on international flights.
- UMNR bookings must be made through the call centre or a ticket office. They cannot be
  completed online.
- The accompanying adult at departure must remain at the airport until the aircraft has
  departed. A named adult with photographic identification must be present at arrival.
- Unaccompanied minors are not accepted on the last flight of the day on any route, and
  are not accepted on itineraries involving a connection of more than 3 hours.

## Infants and Children

- Infants under 2 years travel on an adult's lap at 10 percent of the applicable adult
  fare on domestic sectors and 15 percent on international sectors.
- One adult may carry only one lap infant. A second infant requires a separate seat and a
  child fare, plus an approved car seat.
- Bassinets are available on international sectors only, at bulkhead seats, and must be
  requested 48 hours before departure. Bassinets suit infants up to 11 kg.
- A fully collapsible stroller and a car seat are carried free of charge in addition to
  the baggage allowance for every child under 12.

## Medical Clearance

Medical clearance from the Indus Air medical desk is required at least 72 hours before
departure for passengers who:

- travel on a stretcher or require in-flight oxygen,
- have had surgery within the previous 10 days,
- are in the 36th week of pregnancy or later, or the 32nd week or later for a multiple
  pregnancy,
- have an infectious condition, or a condition that could worsen in a pressurised cabin.

Expectant mothers between the 28th and 35th week must carry a medical certificate issued
within 7 days of travel confirming fitness to fly and the expected delivery date.
Certificates older than 7 days are not accepted at the counter.

## Guide and Service Animals

Trained guide dogs and recognised service animals accompanying a passenger with a
disability travel free of charge in the cabin, without a container, on domestic sectors.
Acceptance on international sectors depends on destination import rules, which remain the
passenger's responsibility to verify. Emotional support animals are not accepted in the
cabin and must travel under the Pets policy.
