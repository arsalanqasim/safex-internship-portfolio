# Indus Air - Baggage Policy

Document owner: Ground Operations
Last reviewed: 2026-07-01
Applies to: all Indus Air marketed and operated flights (IA-prefixed flight numbers)

## Cabin Baggage Allowance

Every passenger travelling on a confirmed ticket may carry one piece of cabin baggage
plus one small personal item into the aircraft cabin.

- Cabin bag limit: 7 kg, maximum dimensions 55 x 40 x 20 cm.
- Personal item: one laptop bag, handbag or camera bag, maximum 40 x 30 x 15 cm.
- Infants travelling on an adult's lap are allowed one additional 5 kg diaper bag.
- Cabin bags that exceed 7 kg are tagged at the gate and moved to the hold at no charge
  on domestic sectors. On international sectors the excess baggage rate applies.

## Checked Baggage Allowance

Checked allowance is determined by the fare family purchased, not by the aircraft type.

| Fare family | Domestic allowance | International allowance |
|---|---|---|
| Indus Saver | 20 kg | 25 kg |
| Indus Flex | 25 kg | 30 kg |
| Indus Business | 35 kg | 40 kg |

- The allowance is a total weight, not a per-piece weight. It may be split across a
  maximum of two pieces on domestic sectors and three pieces on international sectors.
- No single piece may exceed 32 kg. Pieces above 32 kg are refused at the counter for
  ground handler safety reasons and must be repacked.
- Maximum dimensions per checked piece: 158 cm total (length + width + height).

## Excess Baggage Charges

Excess baggage is charged per kilogram above the fare allowance.

- Domestic sectors: PKR 550 per kg.
- Regional international sectors (Gulf, Middle East): PKR 1,400 per kg.
- Excess baggage purchased online through Manage Booking, up to 6 hours before
  departure, is discounted by 20 percent against the airport rate.
- Excess baggage charges are non-refundable once the bag is accepted and tagged.

## Sports and Oversized Equipment

- Golf bags, surfboards, bicycles and ski equipment are accepted as checked baggage.
- Each item counts against the fare allowance and attracts a flat handling fee of
  PKR 3,500 per item on domestic sectors and PKR 7,000 on international sectors.
- Oversized items must be declared at least 24 hours before departure so hold space can
  be confirmed. Undeclared oversized items are carried subject to available space.

## Delayed, Damaged and Lost Baggage

Report all baggage irregularities at the arrival hall Baggage Service Desk before
leaving the airport. A Property Irregularity Report (PIR) reference is issued at that
point and is required for every subsequent claim.

- Delayed baggage: Indus Air traces the bag for 21 days. Passengers away from their home
  city receive an interim expense allowance of PKR 8,000 per day for a maximum of 3 days
  against submitted receipts.
- Baggage not located within 21 days is formally declared lost. Compensation is settled
  at PKR 3,000 per kg of the checked weight recorded on the baggage tag.
- Damaged baggage must be reported within 7 days of arrival. Wheels, handles, straps and
  minor scuffs from normal handling are excluded from the damage policy.
- Claims are filed through Manage Booking or by email to baggage.claims@indusair.example
  with the PIR reference, the baggage tag and the boarding pass.

## Items Not Permitted in Checked Baggage

Cash, jewellery, laptops, mobile phones, medication, passports, keys, and fragile or
perishable goods must travel in cabin baggage. Indus Air accepts no liability for these
categories when they are placed in checked baggage.

Loose lithium batteries and power banks are never permitted in the hold. See the
Restricted Items policy for the full dangerous goods list.
