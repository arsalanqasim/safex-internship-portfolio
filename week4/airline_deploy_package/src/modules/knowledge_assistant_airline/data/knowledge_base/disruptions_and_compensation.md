# Indus Air - Delays, Cancellations and Disruption Care

Document owner: Operations Control Centre
Last reviewed: 2026-07-10
Applies to: all Indus Air operated flights disrupted after ticket issue

## Definitions

- Controllable disruption: a delay or cancellation caused by something within Indus Air's
  control, such as crew rostering, engineering defects, aircraft rotation or commercial
  decisions.
- Uncontrollable disruption: a delay or cancellation caused by weather, air traffic
  control restrictions, airport closure, security incidents, strikes by third parties, or
  government direction.

Duty of care is provided on every disruption. Cash compensation is provided only on
controllable disruptions.

## Duty of Care During Delays

Care is measured from the original scheduled departure time.

| Delay length | Care provided |
|---|---|
| 2 hours or more | Refreshment voucher, free rebooking to a later flight |
| 4 hours or more | Hot meal voucher, two phone calls or equivalent data allowance |
| 6 hours or more | Hotel accommodation and airport transfer where an overnight is required |

Hotel accommodation is provided only when the delay requires an overnight stay and the
passenger is away from their city of residence.

## Compensation for Controllable Disruption

Where Indus Air causes the disruption, passengers receive the following in addition to
duty of care.

- Delay of 3 to 5 hours: PKR 10,000 per passenger on domestic sectors, PKR 22,000 on
  international sectors.
- Delay of more than 5 hours, or a cancellation notified less than 14 days before
  departure: PKR 18,000 per passenger on domestic sectors, PKR 40,000 on international
  sectors.
- Compensation is paid per passenger, including infants holding a ticket.
- Compensation claims must be filed within 60 days of the disrupted flight through
  Manage Booking or by email to care.claims@indusair.example.
- Claims are acknowledged within 5 working days and settled within 30 working days.

No cash compensation is payable on uncontrollable disruptions. Duty of care and the right
to a free rebooking or full refund still apply.

## Passenger Choices When a Flight Is Cancelled

When Indus Air cancels a flight, the passenger chooses one of the following, regardless
of the fare family originally purchased:

1. Rebooking onto the next available Indus Air flight at no additional cost.
2. Rebooking onto a later date of the passenger's choosing within 30 days, at no
   additional cost and with no fare difference collected.
3. A full refund of the unused portion of the ticket to the original form of payment,
   with no cancellation fee, processed within 7 working days.

A passenger who accepts a full refund ends the airline's duty of care from that point.

## Missed Connections

Indus Air protects connections only when both flights are on a single Indus Air ticket.

- Minimum connection time is 60 minutes on domestic to domestic connections and
  90 minutes where an international sector is involved.
- Where a passenger misses a connection because the inbound Indus Air flight arrived late,
  the passenger is rebooked on the next available flight at no charge and receives duty of
  care based on the total delay to final destination.
- Separately purchased tickets are not protected. A missed connection on a separate ticket
  is treated as a no-show on the onward flight.

## Schedule Changes Before Departure

- A schedule change of less than 60 minutes is applied automatically and does not entitle
  the passenger to a refund.
- A schedule change of 60 minutes or more entitles the passenger to a free move to another
  Indus Air flight on the same route within 7 days, or a full refund.
- Passengers are notified of schedule changes by SMS and email to the contact details in
  the booking. Passengers who booked through a travel agent are notified through the agent.
