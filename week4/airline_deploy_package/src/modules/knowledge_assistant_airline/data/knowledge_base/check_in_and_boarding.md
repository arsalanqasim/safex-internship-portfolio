# Indus Air - Check-in, Boarding and Seating

Document owner: Airport Services
Last reviewed: 2026-06-28
Applies to: all Indus Air operated departures

## Online and Mobile Check-in

- Online check-in opens 48 hours before scheduled departure and closes 2 hours before
  departure on domestic sectors and 3 hours before departure on international sectors.
- Passengers travelling with checked baggage still need to drop bags at the airport
  Baggage Drop counter within the counter timings below.
- Online check-in is not available for unaccompanied minors, passengers requiring
  wheelchair assistance, stretcher cases, or groups of 10 passengers or more. These
  categories must check in at the airport counter.

## Airport Counter Timings

| Sector | Counter opens | Counter closes |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic | 3 hours before departure | 45 minutes before departure |
| International | 4 hours before departure | 60 minutes before departure |

Counters close strictly at the stated time. Passengers who arrive after counter closing
are treated as a no-show under the Booking Changes and Refunds policy and are not
accepted for travel, regardless of the reason for late arrival.

## Boarding Gate

- Boarding begins 40 minutes before scheduled departure.
- The boarding gate closes 15 minutes before scheduled departure.
- Passengers who have checked in but do not present at the gate before it closes are
  offloaded, and any checked baggage is removed from the aircraft. Offloaded passengers
  are treated as a no-show.
- Priority boarding is offered to Indus Business passengers, IndusMiles Gold and
  Platinum members, passengers requiring assistance, and families travelling with
  children under 5.

## Seat Selection

- Indus Saver: seat selection is paid. Standard seats PKR 1,200, extra legroom seats
  PKR 3,000, front row seats PKR 3,500 per sector.
- Indus Flex: standard seats are free. Extra legroom and front row seats are charged at
  50 percent of the published rate.
- Indus Business: all seats are free of charge.
- Emergency exit row seats are assigned only to passengers aged 15 to 65 who are
  physically able to operate the exit and who understand safety briefings in English or
  Urdu. Exit rows are never assigned to passengers travelling with infants, unaccompanied
  minors, or passengers requiring assistance.
- Where no seat is selected, the system assigns a seat automatically at check-in.
  Indus Air makes no guarantee that a family or group will be seated together unless
  seats are purchased.

## Denied Boarding and Overbooking

Indus Air may sell more seats than the aircraft holds to account for expected no-shows.
When more passengers present than there are seats:

1. Volunteers are invited to travel on a later flight in exchange for compensation.
2. If insufficient volunteers come forward, boarding is denied first to passengers who
   checked in latest, excluding unaccompanied minors and passengers requiring assistance.
3. Passengers denied boarding involuntarily receive a seat on the next available Indus
   Air flight plus compensation of PKR 20,000 on domestic sectors and PKR 45,000 on
   international sectors.
4. Denied boarding compensation is paid in addition to, and does not replace, the
   passenger's right to a refund of the unused ticket.

## Travel Documents

Passengers are responsible for holding valid travel documents. Indus Air checks documents
at the counter and at the gate but does not advise on visa eligibility. Passengers refused
entry at destination are returned at their own cost, and the outbound ticket is treated as
flown with no refund due.
