# QuickBite Delivery Policy

## Delivery Areas and Coverage
QuickBite currently delivers within a 12-kilometer radius of each partner restaurant in supported cities (Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, and Rawalpindi). You can check whether your address is covered by entering your delivery pin on the app's checkout screen. If a restaurant is outside the coverage zone for your address, it will not appear in your search results.

## Standard Delivery Times
Most orders are delivered within 30-45 minutes of order confirmation during normal hours (10 AM - 11 PM). During peak hours (12-2 PM and 7-9 PM) or bad weather, delivery may take up to 60 minutes. The app shows a live estimated delivery time that updates as your rider moves.

## Delivery Fees
Delivery fees range from PKR 59 to PKR 149 depending on distance between the restaurant and your address. Orders above PKR 1,500 from participating restaurants qualify for free delivery. QuickBite Plus subscribers get free delivery on all orders above PKR 500.

## Late Delivery Compensation
If your order arrives more than 20 minutes after the estimated delivery time shown at checkout, you are automatically eligible for a QuickBite Credit equal to your delivery fee, added to your account within 24 hours. No claim form is required — this is applied automatically based on the rider's GPS-logged delivery timestamp.

## Contactless and In-Person Delivery
You can choose "Leave at door" during checkout for contactless delivery, in which case the rider will take a photo of the order at your doorstep. For in-person delivery, riders will call or message you upon arrival and wait up to 5 minutes at the delivery address.

## Order Tracking
Once a restaurant accepts your order, you can track it live on the map inside the app, including the rider's name, photo, and live location. You will also receive push notifications at each stage: order confirmed, being prepared, picked up, and out for delivery.

## Undeliverable Orders
If a rider cannot reach you after 3 call attempts and 10 minutes of waiting at the delivery address, the order will be marked undeliverable and a partial refund (order value minus preparation and delivery costs) will be issued to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.
