# QuickBite Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling an Order Before Restaurant Acceptance
You can cancel any order for free within 60 seconds of placing it, before the restaurant has accepted it. Go to "My Orders," select the active order, and tap "Cancel Order." The full amount is refunded automatically to your original payment method or QuickBite Wallet.

## Cancelling After Restaurant Acceptance
Once a restaurant has accepted and started preparing your order, cancellation is no longer free. You may still request cancellation through the app, but a cancellation fee equal to 50% of the food subtotal will apply to cover the restaurant's preparation costs. Delivery fees are always refunded in full when you cancel before pickup.

## Refunds for Missing or Incorrect Items
If any item is missing or incorrect when your order arrives, report it within 24 hours via "Help" > "Order Issue" in the app. Attach a photo of what you received if possible. Approved claims are refunded as QuickBite Credit within minutes, or to your original payment method within 3-5 business days if you request a cash refund instead of credit.

## Refunds for Poor Food Quality
Complaints about food quality (spoiled, cold, or not as described) must be reported within 24 hours of delivery. Our support team reviews photo evidence and the restaurant's recent complaint history. Approved refunds cover the affected item(s) only, not the full order, unless the entire order was affected.

## Payment Method Refund Timelines
- QuickBite Wallet / Credit: instant to 1 hour
- Debit/Credit card: 3-7 business days
- JazzCash / EasyPaisa: 1-3 business days
- Cash on Delivery orders: refunded as QuickBite Credit only, since no original payment method exists to refund to

## Refund Abuse Policy
Accounts that file an unusually high number of refund claims relative to their order history may be flagged for manual review. Repeated unsubstantiated claims can result in refunds being limited to QuickBite Credit only, or in serious cases, account suspension. Genuine, well-documented issues are never penalized.

## Disputing a Refund Decision
If your refund request is denied and you believe this was a mistake, you can request a second review by replying to the decision notification within 7 days. A senior support agent (not the original reviewer) will re-examine your case.
