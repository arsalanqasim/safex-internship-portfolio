# Indus Air - Pets, Dangerous Goods and Restricted Items

Document owner: Safety and Compliance
Last reviewed: 2026-07-08
Applies to: all Indus Air operated flights

## Pets in the Cabin

- Only domestic cats and dogs are accepted in the cabin. Birds, rodents, reptiles and
  all other species are refused in the cabin without exception.
- The combined weight of the animal and its carrier must not exceed 8 kg.
- The carrier must be leak proof, ventilated, and no larger than 45 x 30 x 25 cm, and must
  fit fully under the seat in front. The animal must remain inside the carrier for the
  whole flight.
- Cabin pet fee: PKR 9,000 per sector on domestic flights, PKR 18,000 on international.
- A maximum of 3 animals is accepted in the cabin per flight, allocated on a first come
  first served basis at the time of booking.
- Pets are not permitted in Indus Business, in exit rows, or in bulkhead rows fitted with
  a bassinet.

## Pets in the Hold

- Animals above 8 kg combined weight travel in the temperature controlled hold in an
  IATA compliant rigid container.
- Hold pet fee: PKR 16,000 per sector on domestic flights, PKR 32,000 on international.
- Snub nosed breeds, including Persian and Himalayan cats, and Pug, Bulldog, Boxer and
  Shih Tzu dogs, are refused in the hold on all routes. These breeds are at elevated risk
  of respiratory distress and may travel only in the cabin if within the 8 kg limit.
- Hold carriage is suspended on any sector where the forecast ground temperature at
  origin or destination exceeds 32 degrees Celsius or falls below 5 degrees Celsius.

## Documentation for Animals

Every animal, in cabin or hold, requires:

- a valid vaccination record including rabies, administered at least 30 days and no more
  than 12 months before travel,
- a veterinary health certificate issued within 72 hours of departure,
- an import permit for the destination on international sectors.

All animal bookings must be confirmed at least 72 hours before departure through the call
centre. Animals cannot be added to a booking online or at the airport counter.

## Lithium Batteries and Power Banks

- Power banks and spare lithium batteries are permitted in cabin baggage only, never in
  the hold, and must have terminals insulated against short circuit.
- Batteries up to 100 Wh are permitted without approval, with a limit of 20 spare cells
  per passenger.
- Batteries between 100 Wh and 160 Wh require airline approval, with a maximum of 2 spare
  batteries per passenger.
- Batteries above 160 Wh are refused for carriage on all Indus Air flights.
- Devices containing lithium batteries that are subject to a manufacturer safety recall
  are refused in the cabin and in the hold.

## Items Prohibited on All Flights

The following are never accepted in cabin or checked baggage:

- Explosives, fireworks, flares and ammunition beyond 5 kg of securely packed sporting
  cartridges declared and approved in advance.
- Compressed gases, including camping stove cylinders and self inflating rafts.
- Flammable liquids, paints, thinners and lighter fuel.
- Corrosives, including wet cell batteries not fitted to an approved mobility aid.
- Toxic and infectious substances, and radioactive material.
- Electronic cigarettes and vaping devices in checked baggage. These are permitted in the
  cabin but must not be charged or used on board.

## Liquids and Sharp Objects in the Cabin

- Liquids, aerosols and gels in the cabin are limited to containers of 100 ml or less,
  carried in a single transparent resealable bag of 1 litre capacity.
- Medication, baby food and special dietary liquids are exempt from the 100 ml limit in
  reasonable quantities for the journey, subject to security screening.
- Knives, scissors with blades over 6 cm, razor blades and tools over 6 cm must travel in
  checked baggage.
- Duty free liquids purchased airside are permitted in the cabin in a sealed security
  tamper evident bag with the receipt visible inside.
