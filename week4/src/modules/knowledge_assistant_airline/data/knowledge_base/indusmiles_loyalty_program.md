# Indus Air - IndusMiles Loyalty Programme

Document owner: Loyalty and Partnerships
Last reviewed: 2026-06-20
Applies to: enrolled IndusMiles members on Indus Air marketed flights

## Membership Tiers

Tier status is calculated on qualifying miles flown in a rolling 12 month period.
Miles earned from partner spending do not count towards tier qualification.

| Tier | Qualifying miles in 12 months | Earn rate | Extra baggage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blue | 0 | 1 mile per PKR 100 spent | None |
| Silver | 25,000 | 1.25 miles per PKR 100 | 5 kg |
| Gold | 50,000 | 1.5 miles per PKR 100 | 10 kg |
| Platinum | 100,000 | 2 miles per PKR 100 | 15 kg |

Tier status is valid for 12 months from the date it is achieved and is reassessed on the
member's anniversary date.

## Tier Benefits

- Silver: 5 kg extra baggage, priority check-in counter, waived seat selection on
  standard seats.
- Gold: all Silver benefits, plus 10 kg extra baggage, priority boarding, lounge access
  for the member only at Islamabad and Karachi.
- Platinum: all Gold benefits, plus 15 kg extra baggage, lounge access with one guest,
  guaranteed seat on sold out flights when booked at least 72 hours before departure, and
  a waived change fee on Indus Saver tickets.

## Earning Miles

- Miles are credited within 7 working days of flight completion.
- Miles are earned on the base fare only. Government taxes, airport charges and fuel
  surcharges do not earn miles.
- Award tickets and staff tickets do not earn miles.
- Retro-claims for missing miles may be filed up to 6 months after the flight date, with
  the boarding pass and ticket number as supporting evidence.

## Spending Miles

- Award tickets start at 12,000 miles for a one-way domestic sector in economy and
  30,000 miles for a one-way regional international sector.
- Award seats are capacity controlled. Indus Air does not guarantee award availability on
  any given flight or date.
- Government taxes and airport charges are always payable in cash on award tickets and
  cannot be covered by miles.
- Miles may also be used for seat selection, excess baggage and lounge passes through
  Manage Booking.

## Mile Expiry and Account Rules

- Miles expire 36 months after the month in which they were earned.
- Any qualifying flight activity extends the expiry of the whole balance by a further
  36 months from the date of that activity.
- Miles are not transferable between members and have no cash value.
- Family pooling is available for up to 6 members of one household, who may combine miles
  into a single redemption account. Tier status is never pooled and remains individual.
- An account inactive for 24 consecutive months is suspended. Suspended accounts are
  reinstated on request within 12 months, after which the balance is forfeited.
