# Indus Air - Booking Changes, Cancellations and Refunds

Document owner: Revenue and Distribution
Last reviewed: 2026-07-05
Applies to: tickets issued on the Indus Air website, mobile app, call centre and
appointed travel agents

## Fare Families

Change and refund rights depend entirely on the fare family shown on the ticket.

| Fare family | Date change | Cancellation | Seat selection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indus Saver | Permitted with fee | Non-refundable, taxes only | Paid |
| Indus Flex | One free change | Refundable with fee | Free standard seats |
| Indus Business | Unlimited free changes | Fully refundable | Free, all seats |

## Date and Flight Changes

- Changes must be requested at least 3 hours before scheduled departure on domestic
  sectors and 4 hours before departure on international sectors.
- Indus Saver change fee: PKR 4,000 domestic, PKR 9,000 international, plus any
  difference in fare between the original and the new booking.
- Indus Flex includes one free change. Subsequent changes are charged at PKR 2,500 plus
  fare difference. The fare difference is always payable even on a free change.
- Indus Business tickets change without a fee. Fare difference still applies when moving
  to a higher priced booking class.
- Name changes are not permitted on any fare family. A ticket is valid only for the
  passenger named at the time of issue and must be cancelled and rebooked if the
  traveller changes.

## Cancellations and Refund Amounts

- Indus Saver is non-refundable. Passengers who cancel receive a refund of government
  taxes and airport charges only, with no refund of the base fare.
- Indus Flex is refundable against a cancellation fee of PKR 6,000 domestic and
  PKR 12,000 international, deducted from the base fare.
- Indus Business is fully refundable to the original form of payment with no fee.
- Passengers who do not cancel before departure and do not travel are recorded as a
  no-show. No-show tickets forfeit the base fare on every fare family, including
  Indus Business, and refund only unused government taxes.

## Refund Processing Times

Refunds are always returned to the original form of payment. Indus Air does not
transfer refunds to a third party account.

- Credit and debit card refunds: 7 to 10 working days from approval.
- Bank transfer and internet banking refunds: 10 to 14 working days.
- Cash bookings made at an Indus Air ticket office: refunded by bank transfer within
  14 working days once bank details are verified.
- Travel agent bookings are refunded to the issuing agent, who then settles with the
  passenger. Indus Air cannot refund an agency ticket directly to the passenger.

## 24 Hour Cancellation Window

Any ticket booked directly with Indus Air may be cancelled within 24 hours of issue for
a full refund with no fee, on every fare family, provided departure is at least 7 days
away. This window does not apply to travel agent bookings or to group bookings of 10
passengers or more.

## Travel Credit

Passengers who prefer not to take a cash refund may convert the ticket value into an
Indus Air travel credit.

- Travel credit is issued at 110 percent of the refundable ticket value.
- Credit is valid for 12 months from the date of issue and may be used across multiple
  bookings until exhausted.
- Travel credit is issued in the name of the original passenger and is not transferable.
- Travel credit cannot be converted back into a cash refund once accepted.
