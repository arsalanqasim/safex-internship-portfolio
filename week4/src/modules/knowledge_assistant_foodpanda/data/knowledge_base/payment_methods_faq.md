# QuickBite Payment Methods FAQ

## Accepted Payment Methods
QuickBite accepts Cash on Delivery (COD), Visa/Mastercard debit and credit cards, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and the in-app QuickBite Wallet. Bank transfers and cheques are not supported for order payments.

## QuickBite Wallet
The QuickBite Wallet lets you pre-load funds for faster checkout and stores any refunds issued as credit. You can top up the wallet using a card, JazzCash, or EasyPaisa, in amounts from PKR 500 to PKR 20,000 per transaction. Wallet balance never expires and can be used for any order, including delivery fees.

## Applying Promo Codes
Enter a promo code in the "Promo Code" field at checkout before placing your order; codes cannot be applied retroactively after an order is placed. Each promo code has its own minimum order value, applicable restaurants, and expiry date, shown when you enter the code. Only one promo code can be applied per order.

## Cash on Delivery Limits
Cash on Delivery is available for orders up to PKR 10,000. For orders above this amount, you must pay with a card, wallet, JazzCash, or EasyPaisa. Riders carry limited change, so please try to have close to the exact amount ready.

## Failed or Duplicate Card Payments
If a card payment fails but money was deducted from your account, it is automatically reversed by your bank within 5-7 business days; QuickBite does not hold these funds. If you were charged twice for the same order due to a network issue, contact support with both transaction IDs and the duplicate charge will be refunded within 3 business days after verification.

## Saved Cards and Security
Card details are tokenized and stored securely by our PCI-DSS compliant payment partner; QuickBite does not store your full card number. You can remove a saved card anytime from "Payment Methods" in your account settings.

## Loyalty Points
Every paid order (excluding delivery fees) earns 1 loyalty point per PKR 100 spent. Points can be redeemed for discount vouchers starting at 200 points = PKR 100 off. Points expire 12 months after being earned if unused.
