# QuickBite Rider Partner FAQ

## Becoming a QuickBite Rider
To become a rider, you must be at least 18 years old, own a motorbike or bicycle in working condition, have a valid CNIC, and (for motorbike riders) a valid driving license. Apply through the "QuickBite Rider" app by submitting your documents for verification, which typically takes 2-3 business days.

## Required Equipment
Riders must have a smartphone (Android 8.0+ or iOS 13+) with a working data connection, an insulated delivery bag (provided at a subsidized cost during onboarding), and a valid vehicle registration document if riding a motorbike.

## How Riders Are Paid
Riders earn a base fee per delivery (distance-based, PKR 60-140) plus 100% of any customer tip. Peak-hour deliveries (12-2 PM, 7-9 PM) earn an additional PKR 20-40 surge bonus per order. Weekly earnings are deposited directly to the rider's bank account or JazzCash/EasyPaisa account every Monday for the previous Monday-Sunday period.

## Rider Incentive Programs
Riders who complete 60+ deliveries in a week qualify for a weekly completion bonus of PKR 1,500-3,000 depending on the city. New riders receive a first-30-days bonus structure with guaranteed minimum weekly earnings, provided they complete at least 40 deliveries per week during that period.

## Working Hours and Flexibility
Riders can log in and go online whenever they choose; there are no fixed shifts. However, riders who consistently go online only during low-demand hours may receive fewer order offers, since order distribution is optimized around real-time demand patterns.

## Rider Support and Safety
Riders have access to 24/7 in-app support and an SOS button that alerts the QuickBite Safety Team and shares live location with emergency contacts. QuickBite provides accidental injury insurance coverage for riders while on an active delivery.

## Rider Rating and Deactivation Policy
Riders are rated by customers after each delivery. A rider whose rating falls below 4.0 (out of 5) over their last 100 deliveries will receive a warning and access to a coaching session. Ratings below 3.5, or serious violations (safety issues, order tampering, fraud), can result in temporary suspension or permanent deactivation after review.
