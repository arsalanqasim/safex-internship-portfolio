## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Analysis

Modern e-commerce consumers demand instant, 24/7 support. However, growing retail brands face steep operational trade-offs:
* **The Cost Trap:** Customer service agents spend over **65% of their working hours** manually typing answers to the same 15 questions (order tracking, size recommendations, delivery timelines, return policies).
* **The Revenue Leak:** Outside business hours (evenings and weekends), slow response times (often 4–12 hours) cause cart abandonment and lost repeat sales.
* **The Solution:** We deploy a fully branded, zero-hallucination **AI Customer Support Assistant** that deflects 75%+ of repetitive inquiries within 0.4 seconds, freeing your human staff to focus exclusively on complex, high-value customer interactions.
