## 4. Key Lessons & Outreach Insights

1. **Specific Observations Outperform Generic Templates:** Mentioning an exact friction point (e.g., weekend inquiry delays on Instagram) tripled our response rate compared to standard template blasts.
2. **Interactive Live Portfolio is the Strongest Closer:** Prospects praised the ability to test the chatbot and admin panel directly on Streamlit Cloud before committing to a discovery call.
3. **Multi-Touch Sequences are Essential:** Over 40% of responses came from the 3-day follow-up email rather than the initial touchpoint.
